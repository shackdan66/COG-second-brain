# Welcome to Your COG Second Brain, Dan!

Your COG is personalized for your work as an AWS Cloud consultant at Slalom. Your profile and preferences are stored in editable Markdown files.

## Your Profile Documents

- **[[MY-PROFILE]]**: Your profile, role pack, and workflow preferences
- **[[MY-INTERESTS]]**: Topics for your daily briefs
- **[[MY-INTEGRATIONS]]**: Your active and disabled integrations

You can edit these files anytime. COG reads them when you use skills.

## Skills for Your Role

Your profile uses the Custom role pack. These core skills are a useful starting point:

1. **daily-brief**: Follow AWS, infrastructure, Terraform, EKS, migration, and applied AI developments.
2. **braindump**: Capture technical learnings, consulting observations, and ideas.
3. **url-dump**: Save technical references and extract useful takeaways.
4. **knowledge-consolidation**: Build reusable references from your notes and learnings.
5. **weekly-checkin**: Review what you learned and where to grow next.
6. **update-cog**: Check for updates to the COG framework.

## Your Integrations

**Active**: GitHub

**Disabled**: Slack, Linear, Notion, PostHog, ElevenLabs

Edit [[MY-INTEGRATIONS]] to change these settings.

## Quick Start

### Daily Brief
Run `/daily-brief` for current intelligence on your selected topics: AWS cloud infrastructure, Terraform, EKS/Kubernetes, AWS MGN and migration, practical AI, and skills for cloud consultants.

### Capture a Learning
Use `/braindump` to capture a technical discovery, project thought, or skill you want to develop.

### Save a Reference
Use `/url-dump` with a technical article or documentation link to save it with extracted takeaways.

### Weekly Reflection
Run `/weekly-checkin` to review your progress and spot useful themes across your notes.
